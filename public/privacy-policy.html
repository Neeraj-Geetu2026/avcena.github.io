Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026

AVCENA Gardening & Lawnmowing respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information we collect from you. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you contact us, request a quote, or use our website.

We may collect personal information such as:

  • Your name, phone number, email address, and property address

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your quote request or enquiry

3. Information sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share your information only where necessary to provide our services, with trusted service providers such as email, hosting, or technical support providers, or where required by law.

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted over the internet.

5. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies or basic analytics tools to improve functionality and understand traffic patterns. These tools may collect non-identifying information such as pages visited and general device information. You can adjust your browser settings to disable cookies if you prefer.

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to any legal obligations we may have to retain certain records. If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.
